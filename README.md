### 02. KnockMyRide

**SaaS · QR-Based Vehicle Contact System**

[![Live](https://img.shields.io/badge/Live%20Project-000000?style=flat-square\&logo=vercel\&logoColor=white)](https://knockmyride.com)

A privacy-focused SaaS product that allows people to contact vehicle owners through a QR code without exposing the owner's personal phone number.

The platform is designed for everyday vehicle-related situations such as parking conflicts, emergencies, lights left on, lost items, and other situations where contacting a vehicle owner is necessary.

**Highlights**

* QR-based vehicle identification
* Privacy-focused communication
* No mobile application required
* No registration required for scanners
* Call and WhatsApp communication
* Support for multiple vehicle types
* Vehicle management
* SaaS-oriented architecture
* Planned analytics and fleet-management capabilities

**Stack:** `Next.js` `TypeScript` `Node.js` `MongoDB` `Redis` `QR Technology`
